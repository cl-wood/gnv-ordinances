ImagineGNV.txt is the city of Gainesville, Florida's comprehensive plan draft.

IMPROVEMENTS.md contains recommendations to make the comprehensive plan more housing-friendly.

## Task
Review ImagineGNV.txt and identify ALL provisions that restrict housing supply. Add each to IMPROVEMENTS.md with proposed replacement language.

## What restricts housing supply?
Flag provisions that:
- Limit density or unit counts
- Impose parking minimums
- Restrict housing types (ADUs, duplexes, missing middle)
- Add cost through excessive requirements
- Slow down or complicate approval processes
- Mandate large lot sizes or setbacks
- Restrict where housing can be built

## Format for each recommendation
Use this structure:

### [Descriptive title]

**Current policy:** Policy X.X.X states: "[quote or paraphrase]"

**Problem:** [Explain in plain language how this restricts housing supply]

**Proposed replacement:** "[New policy language]"

## Rules
- Cite by policy number (e.g., "Policy 1.5.8"), never by line number
- Write for a general public audience—avoid jargon
- Focus solely on increasing housing supply; do not weigh other concerns
- Use valid markdown formatting
